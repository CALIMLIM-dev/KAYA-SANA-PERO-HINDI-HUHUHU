KAYA is a job app for the Philippines. Workers (tradespeople such as plumbers, electricians, carpenters, cleaners, painters) find jobs; employers (people and registered businesses) post jobs and hire. Job payment is agreed and paid directly between the two people; KAYA never holds or moves job money.

ACCOUNTS
- Sign up with an email or phone number and a password, or with Google. Then set up a worker profile, an employer profile, or both.
- One account can both look for work and hire: set up both profiles and switch with the Worker and Employer buttons in the search bar on the home screen. Exception: a registered business (company) account cannot also have a worker profile.
- Remove a profile or delete the account: Profile, Settings, then the last row. With both profiles, one can be removed and the other kept. An employer profile with open job posts has to close them first. Deleting the account asks for the password and cannot be undone. It is refused while hired on a job in progress or while having a worker on one.

VERIFICATION
- Verified means an admin checked a Philippine government ID and a selfie of the person holding it. A business is verified by its registration paper instead: a DTI certificate, an SEC registration or a Mayor's permit. The badge confirms who someone is, not how good their work is.
- How: open the worker or employer profile from the Profile tab, go to Verification, upload the documents. An admin reviews them and the person gets a notification either way. If refused, the notification says why and they can upload again.
- Before verification you can browse jobs, profiles and the community board. Posting a job, applying, inviting, accepting an invitation, posting or commenting on the community board, claiming free Barya and topping up all need a verified account.

LOOKING FOR WORK
- Apply: open the job and tap Apply Now. The price in Barya is on the button. The Barya comes back if you withdraw within an hour of applying, or if the job is deleted or its post ends while you are still waiting.
- Applications and invitations: Home, then My Applications.
- When hired, a chat opens with the employer. Agree on the day there: either side can propose a schedule and the other accepts it. During the job the worker can share live location with the employer and stop any time.
- Ranking: employers see first the workers who have the skills a job asks for, or who work in its trade. Within that, a fuller profile ranks higher: reviews, jobs done, experience, certificates and being verified. Topping up does not move anyone up the list. A boost puts a profile at the top of the feed for a few days.

HIRING
- Post a job: tap Post a Job on the home screen. There is no start date: the post goes up when it is posted and stays up until the deadline you pick. The first days are free; keeping it up longer costs Barya and needs an account that has topped up. The price shows under the deadline while picking it. The day of the work is agreed in the chat after hiring.
- Find workers: post a job and workers apply, or search workers by trade and distance and invite one. Inviting costs Barya; inviting someone hired before costs less. An account that has topped up also sees the list of matching workers for each job.
- See applicants: Home, My Jobs, then Applicants on the job. Accept or reject from there.
- A job can need from one to twenty workers; the post takes applicants until every spot is filled.
- Edit a job: Home, My Jobs, Manage, then Edit. You pay only for days or a boost you add.

FINISHING A JOB
- When the work is done, both people tap Mark as Complete. It is complete once both have.
- The due day is the end date on the post, or the day agreed in the chat's schedule if there is one; the agreed schedule wins, so a job finished early can be completed early. Before the due day, one person's Mark as Complete is a request that waits for the other.
- Seven days after the due day: if one side confirmed and the other said nothing, the job counts as completed. If neither confirmed, it closes as not completed for both. Reminders are sent on the due day and before it settles.
- If the other side marked it complete but the work did not happen or was not done, tap Report a Problem on the hire. That pauses it until KAYA reviews it.
- Cancel Job: either side can cancel a hire and give a reason. Before the start day, or if the other side agrees, it is cancelled at once; otherwise the other side has 48 hours, then it is cancelled anyway.
- Reviews: once a job is complete, each side can review the other once, for a limited time. A review cannot be edited after it is sent. An admin can hide a review that breaks the rules.
- A finished job's chat is archived. It comes back if the same people work together again.

BARYA AND PAYMENT
- Barya is KAYA's credit. It has no cash value. Claim free Barya in My Wallet when starting and again each month, and earn some with badges.
- Costs Barya: applying, inviting, boosting, keeping a job post up past its free days, and posting on the community board. Messaging and commenting are free.
- Top up: Profile, My Wallet, then choose a package. While KAYA is in testing, top-ups are free and the Barya is added at once. Topping up needs a verified account.
- A top-up of any size unlocks: boosting, keeping job posts up past the free days, the list of matching workers for your jobs, up to three community posts at a time instead of one, and your card opening as a resume on an employer's Applicants list.
- Refunds of Barya happen automatically in set cases; the Refund Policy is in Settings, About & Legal, and linked at the bottom of My Wallet.
- Paying a worker: directly, as agreed in the chat. KAYA does not hold job payments.

COMMUNITY BOARD
- The Community tab. Workers post that they are available; employers and businesses post that they are hiring. Anyone signed in can read and like posts; verified accounts can comment.
- Every post is read by KAYA before it goes up, and its paid days start when it is approved. If it is not approved the person is told why and the Barya is returned.
- One post at a time, or three once the account has topped up. Posts waiting for review count.

CHAT RULES
- Chats, comments and job posts are read by a filter. Swear words, phone numbers, email addresses and asking to move to another app are hidden with asterisks; the message still sends. This keeps people from being taken off KAYA and scammed.

REPORTING
- Report buttons: on a person's profile (the flag at the top), on a job post, in a chat (menu at the top right, Report User), on a community post or comment, and under a review about you on your own profile. Up to three photos can be added as evidence.
- The reported person is not told who reported them, and is not told about the report unless KAYA finds a violation.
- The person who reported is told the outcome: action was taken, no violation was found, or the report was found to be false. Filing false reports is itself a violation.

VIOLATIONS, SUSPENSIONS AND APPEALS
- A violation is given only by a KAYA admin after a review. The penalty depends on how serious the violation is and how many violations are still counting on the account:
  - Minor (for example spam, a fake review, misuse of custom categories): first a warning, then a 7-day suspension, then a ban.
  - Serious (for example not showing up, marking a job done when it was not, a misleading job post, getting back at a reporter): first a 7-day suspension, then a ban.
  - Critical (scam or fraud, fake documents or identity, harassment or threats): a ban.
- A violation counts for 180 days.
- Account Status (Profile, Settings, Account Status) shows each violation, what it means and when a suspension ends.
- Appeal: open Account Status, choose the violation, tap Appeal and give the reason, within 30 days of the violation. A KAYA admin reviews it; the appeal is accepted (the violation is removed) or refused (it stays). The assistant cannot decide or speed up an appeal.
- A suspended or banned account can still open Account Status, appeal, and message KAYA Support.

SUPPORT
- This chat is KAYA Support. KAYA Assistant answers first. Tapping Talk to a Person, or asking for one, passes the chat to a KAYA team member, who answers in this same chat. Back to KAYA Assistant returns to the assistant.
- The chat is temporary: it is erased when the person leaves it. A chat waiting for the team is kept until the team replies and the reply has been read.
